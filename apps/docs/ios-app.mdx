The Roomote iOS app puts your Sessions and tasks in your pocket. It shows the
same Sessions you see on the web, streams replies live, and sends a push
notification when Roomote needs you: a question to answer, a capability to
approve, a task that finished, or a reply that landed while you were away.
Questions and approvals can be answered straight from the notification.

## One app per deployment

Like its Slack app, each Roomote deployment builds and distributes its own copy
of the iOS app. There is no shared App Store listing that talks to every
deployment; your build is tied to your Apple Developer team, your bundle
identifier, and your deployment URL, and push notifications are signed with
your own Apple Push Notification service (APNs) key. Roomote never sees that
key: it is stored encrypted on your deployment's control plane and used only
there.

Setting the app up takes four steps:

1. Enroll in the Apple Developer Program.
2. Create an APNs key and an app identifier.
3. Enter them in **Settings > Integrations > iOS app**.
4. Build the app and distribute it through TestFlight.

## 1. Apple Developer enrollment

You need a paid [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/)
membership (an organization membership if the app will be shared with a team).
Note your **Team ID** from **Membership details**; it is a 10-character
identifier such as `ABCDE12345`.

## 2. APNs key and app identifier

In **Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles**:

- Under **Identifiers**, register an **App ID** with an explicit bundle
  identifier of your choosing, for example `com.example.roomote`. Enable the
  **Push Notifications** and **Associated Domains** capabilities.
- Under **Keys**, create a key with **Apple Push Notifications service (APNs)**
  enabled. Download the `AuthKey_<Key ID>.p8` file right away; Apple only lets
  you download it once. Note the 10-character **Key ID**.

One APNs key works for both the TestFlight (production APNs) and Xcode debug
(sandbox APNs) builds of the app; the app tells your deployment which
environment each device belongs to when it registers.

## 3. Deployment settings

A deployment admin opens **Settings > Integrations > iOS app** and enters:

- **Team ID**
- **Key ID**
- **Bundle ID**, the identifier you registered above
- **APNs key**, the full contents of the `.p8` file

The key is stored encrypted. Editing the connection later lets you change the
identifiers and leave the key field blank to keep the stored key. Disconnecting
the integration stops all push delivery.

Once saved, the deployment also serves the Apple universal-links manifest at
`/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`, so links to `/sessions/<id>` and
`/task/<id>` on your deployment host open in the app instead of the browser
when the build's associated domain matches.

## 4. Building and distributing

The app source lives in `apps/ios` of the Roomote repository. The release
script bakes your deployment URL and bundle identifier into the build,
archives it, and uploads it to App Store Connect:

```bash
apps/ios/scripts/release.sh
```

Run it on a Mac with Xcode installed; the script's header documents the
settings it needs (your team, bundle identifier, and deployment URL). When the
upload finishes, add testers in App Store Connect under **TestFlight** and
they receive an invitation to install the app.

TestFlight builds expire **90 days** after upload. Re-run the release script
before then to ship a fresh build; testers update in place and keep their
sign-in and device registration.

## Signing in

The app signs in against your deployment. Email and password accounts sign in
natively; single sign-on accounts (Slack, Microsoft, and any other provider)
sign in through a short browser handoff and come back to the app. Sessions
last 30 days and renew on use.

## Notification categories

Each device can turn categories on or off in the app's settings:

| Category | When it fires | Actions from the notification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input needed | Roomote asks you a question in a Session | Reply inline, or open the Session |
| Capability offer | Roomote offers to set up or use a capability | Approve or decline |
| Task finished | A task you started from the web completed, failed, or was canceled | Open the task |
| Reply | Roomote replied in a Session while you were away | Open the Session |

Push notifications are additive to your existing chat notifications: if you
also receive Roomote's direct messages in Slack, Teams, Telegram, or Discord,
those continue unchanged. A notification is not sent while you have the
Session open in a browser.
