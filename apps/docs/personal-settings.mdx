Personal Settings are for the Roomote preferences and accounts that belong to
you, not the whole deployment.

Admins use the other settings pages to connect tools and configure shared
behavior. Individual teammates use Personal Settings to link their own
identities, adjust the app, and manage profile details.

## Open your profile

Use **Profile** to update account details such as your name, email,
avatar, and security settings.

These settings affect your own Roomote account. They do not change deployment
membership, environment access, or integration setup for the rest of the team.

### Set a password

If you signed in through a communications or source-control provider, you can
select **Set password** to add email-and-password sign-in to the same Roomote
account. After setting a password, you can update it from Personal Settings.

## Link your accounts

Use **Linked Accounts** to connect your personal identity for tools your
deployment has enabled.

You may see rows for:

- your source-control provider
- your communications provider
- Linear
- user-linked integrations such as docs, issue trackers, databases, or
  analytics tools

If an integration is not listed, it usually means an admin has not enabled it
for the deployment yet, or the integration does not require personal linking.

## Manage personal integrations

Use **Personal integrations** to add, edit, or revoke API-key integrations that
only your Sessions and coding tasks can use. Roomote keeps the key server-side;
agents receive an opaque reference or a task-scoped substitute rather than the
key itself.

An integration-key approval created from a Session also appears here when you
choose **Only me**. Deployment-wide integration keys instead appear under the
administrator's **Settings → Integrations** page. If an administrator changes a
shared integration to owner-only access, Roomote moves it to that owner's
Personal integrations card.

See [Integration keys](/integration-keys) for approval methods, security
boundaries, task behavior, and self-hosting options.

## Manage personal MCP servers

Use **Personal MCP servers** to add remote MCP servers that only your Sessions
and coding tasks can use, for example a service you authorize with your own
account. Roomote reaches the server through its proxy and keeps headers and
OAuth tokens server-side. A server you ask Roomote to connect privately in a
Session appears here too. Open a server to edit it, manage which tools agents
may use, or share it with everyone in the deployment; Roomote then moves it to
**Settings → Integrations**.

See [Custom MCP servers](/integrations/custom-mcp-servers) for authentication
options and security boundaries.

## Why personal linking matters

Personal linked accounts help Roomote act with the right context. Depending on
the integration, linking can help Roomote:

- associate your chat, source-control, or Linear identity with the Roomote
  task
- use your user-scoped connection for tools that require individual
  authorization
- route follow-up activity back to the right person or thread

Deployment-level setup and personal linking are different. An admin may enable
an integration once, and then each teammate links their own account only when
they need to use it.

## Adjust the app

Personal Settings also include app preferences such as:

- **Color Theme** for light, dark, or system-defined appearance
- **Mind Reader Mode** to expand LLM thoughts by default in task conversations;
  you can still collapse or expand individual thought messages
- **Narration Mode** for a more streamlined task conversation view
- **How to work with me** for private personal instructions and conversational
  learning

Most teammates only need profile, linked accounts, and theme settings.

### Try personalized Home suggestions

**Home suggestions** is a deployment-wide experiment. When an admin enables it
under **Settings > Experimental**, Roomote uses up to
five of your recent successfully completed and ingested task memories to prepare
short ideas for what to do next in the Home composer. Suggestions are prepared
in the background when possible and rotate until you focus the composer; press
Tab to accept the current suggestion.

Only your visible, manually initiated work is eligible. Roomote excludes other
members' work, automations, system tasks, hidden or deleted tasks, incomplete
runs, and memories that have not finished ingesting. If Memory or helper-model
generation is unavailable, Home keeps the standard non-personalized suggestions.
An admin can turn the experiment off to stop personalized suggestion requests
for every member.

### Continue peer conversations

**Peer conversations** is a deployment-wide experiment controlled by admins
under **Settings > Experimental**. When enabled, eligible human replies in
established Slack or Discord session threads can continue reaching Roomote after
someone mentions another participant. Roomote is instructed to stay quiet around
peer discussion unless Roomote is addressed directly or the context clearly
calls for its response. Direct Roomote mentions work normally with the experiment
on or off.

The experiment does not monitor whole channels. Discord supplies messages from
the established thread and resolved user mentions. Telegram is not included:
bot privacy settings can prevent ambient group messages from reaching Roomote,
and Telegram sessions are isolated per linked sender. Microsoft Teams
is also not included because its channel sessions are isolated per
linked sender; webhook delivery and delegated Graph history are not a single
shared, owner-bound session signal.

## Personalize how Roomote works with you

Use **How to work with me** for durable context and preferences that can help
Roomote work with you in the future. This can include recurring
responsibilities, workflows, tools, constraints, and collaboration patterns,
along with your preferred name, language, formality, answer length, use of
examples, directness, update frequency, or accessibility and presentation
preferences you choose to share. These instructions apply to conversations
with you; they do not silently rewrite code or customer-facing deliverables.

**Learn from conversations** is on by default. Roomote may save durable work
context or a preference you state explicitly and may form modest, revisable
style preferences from repeated behavior. It does not save one-off task
details or facts about other people as personal context. Turning learning off
stops new automatic updates but keeps the instructions already shown in the
text box active. **Reset** clears both edited and learned instructions. A reset
does not rebuild them from older conversation history.

Roomote captures each participant's personalization when they first speak in a
conversation and keeps that private snapshot for their later turns in the same
conversation. New preferences stated in a message still guide that request and
are saved for future conversations, but they do not rewrite the current
conversation's snapshot. Edits and resets in Personal Settings likewise apply
to new conversations, or to a participant who has not spoken in the current
conversation yet.

Personalization follows the trusted account associated with the current
speaker on supported chat surfaces and with the person who requested a task.
In a shared thread, Roomote adapts its conversation to the current speaker
while preserving the original requester's task requirements when they conflict.

Personalization is encrypted at rest and is not available to other members or
admins through Roomote's normal UI or API. It is excluded from shared task
transcripts, summaries, memory, and ordinary tool output. It remains subject to
the deployment operator's infrastructure access, backups, and retention
policies. Roomote does not use public-web or LinkedIn enrichment for this
feature.

## Common issues

- **A linked account is missing.** Ask an admin to enable the integration from
  **Settings > Integrations**.
- **A task uses the wrong identity.** Check that the relevant account is
  linked and that you started the task from the expected chat, source-control,
  or dashboard user.
- **You cannot link an account.** Try again after confirming pop-ups and
  redirects are allowed for the Roomote app.
